## MikuMikuDance (PMX)

The six GitHub ZIPs above are for DAZ Studio or Blender, not MikuMikuDance. If you need a PMX, use the separate editions on Renderoverse. Extract the ZIP before opening the PMX, and keep its texture folder alongside it.

- [Delia (Ash's mom) - Pokemon [MMD]](https://renderoverse.com/model/delia-ash-s-mom-pokemon-mmd)
- [Hana Uzaki - Uzaki-chan Wants to Hang Out! [MMD]](https://renderoverse.com/model/hana-uzaki-uzaki-chan-wants-to-hang-out-mmd)
- [Yanagi - the H verse GameCG [MMD]](https://renderoverse.com/model/yanagi-the-h-verse-gamecg-mmd)
- [Mother Gothel - Tangled - MMD](https://renderoverse.com/model/mother-gothel-tangled-mmd-c03) — free high-poly FK rig with 14 textures. Tested in Blender with MMD Tools; standalone MikuMikuDance playback has not been tested.
- [Seigenki - Urotsukidōji: Legend of the Overfiend - MMD](https://renderoverse.com/model/seigenki-urotsukidoji-mmd-c06) — free high-poly FK rig with eight textures. Tested in Blender with MMD Tools; standalone MikuMikuDance playback has not been tested.
- [Drasna - Pokémon - MMD](https://renderoverse.com/model/drasna-pokemon-mmd)
- [Genyo - Urotsukidōji: Legend of the Overfiend - MMD](https://renderoverse.com/model/genyo-urotsukidoji-mmd-solo80)
- [Mitsuki Bakugo - My Hero Academia - MMD](https://renderoverse.com/model/mitsuki-bakugo-mha-mmd-r12)

The model pages include the original full-body previews.

**Not sure which VMD you downloaded?** Open the [MMD Motion Checker](https://huggingface.co/spaces/zuraxe/mmd-motion-checker), leave the PMX field empty, and choose **Inspect VMD only**. Camera keyframes move the camera; bone and morph tracks animate the model. For a name match, add the PMX and choose **Compare track names**. Files stay in your browser. The checker reads tracks; it doesn’t play the motion.

**Need an offline copy?** [Download the MMD Motion Checker ZIP](https://github.com/kruqond/renderoverse-3d-assets/releases/download/mmd-motion-checker-offline-v1.0.0/Renderoverse-MMD-Motion-Checker-Offline-v1.0.0.zip), unzip it, and open `index.html` in your browser. No account or internet connection is needed after download. The [release](https://github.com/kruqond/renderoverse-3d-assets/releases/tag/mmd-motion-checker-offline-v1.0.0) includes a checksum. The ZIP contains the checker and instructions, not character models or motion files.
